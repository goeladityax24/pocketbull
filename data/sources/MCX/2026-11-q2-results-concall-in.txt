MCX Q2 FY27 results - Revenue doubles, record options volumes, margins expand

Revenue: ₹800 crore (+113.9% YoY, +14.0% QoQ)
EBITDA: ₹664 crore (+145.9% YoY, +22.1% QoQ)
PAT: ₹455 crore (+130.8% YoY, +10.1% QoQ)

• Options ADT surged 224.8% YoY to ₹12,00,129 crore

• Futures ADT rose 50.6% YoY to ₹62,819 crore

• Options traded clients more than doubled to 13.03 lakh

• EBITDA margin expanded to 78.0% from 67.3% a year ago

• Tax expense jumped to ₹185 crore, slowing PAT growth QoQ

Outlook: Management plans to broaden participation, expand products and invest in technology.

Disc: Not a buy / sell recommendation.
