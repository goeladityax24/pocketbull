Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited Q2 & H1 FY27 Concall Highlights:

👉Q2 & H1 FY27 & Future Outlook :
▫️FY27 guidance maintained: ~20% revenue growth and ~25% EBITDA growth, with a range of 5 percentage points either side

▫️Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹280 Cr, up 43% YoY; H1 FY27 revenue ₹498 Cr, up 25% YoY
💠Q2 includes ₹53 Cr milestone revenue from complex injectables
💠Balance milestone revenue expected to be recognised in each of the upcoming quarters

▫️EBITDA (excluding one-time IPO expense of ~₹3 Cr): Q2 ₹94 Cr vs ₹50 Cr, up 88% YoY; H1 ₹140 Cr, up 29% YoY

▫️PAT: Q2 ₹52 Cr, up 169% YoY; H1 ₹66 Cr, up 34% YoY

▫️Gross margin: Q2 71.6% vs 64.1%; H1 68.8% vs 63.0%

▫️New businesses in H1: opex ₹57 Cr including ANDA filing fees, depreciation ₹24 Cr
💠Revenue from new businesses expected to commence in the next 6–12 months

▫️Milestone payments are recurring in nature: not linear quarter on quarter but similar year on year
💠Expected to continue consistently through the rest of FY27 and next year
💠Sales and profit share to start before milestones taper off

▫️Management view: Injectables and CDMO can scale to a size comparable to the API business over the coming years

▫️Return profile targeted in new businesses (no distinction between bio-pharma and bio-manufacturing): ROC of at least 30%, EBITDA margin 30–40%, gross margin 70–80%

👉Order book / projects and pipeline:
▫️API business:
💠Q2 revenue ₹227 Cr, up 16% YoY; H1 ₹442 Cr, up 11% YoY, led by volume and product mix
💠Gross margin above 60%; EBITDA margin ~30%; Q2 API EBITDA ₹71 Cr, H1 ₹137 Cr
💠Management expects high single digit to low double digit growth over the visible future
💠60+ molecules in steroids and hormones, 44 USFDA DMFs, 25 European CEPs, presence across top 10 steroid and hormonal API molecules
💠EIRs received from USFDA for Rau and Pithampur SEZ; EU GMP certification for both sites

▫️Classical fermentation APIs (Pithampur SEZ):
💠A couple of products already commercialised for the Indian market; DMFs and CEPs being filed
💠Six more products developed in pipeline; portfolio of half a dozen to a dozen products planned
💠2–3 years before entry into regulated markets

▫️Vegan vitamin D3:
💠Plant and qualification to complete by end of FY27; sales to nutraceutical and veterinary markets from early FY28
💠Pharma-grade D3 needs about another year to qualify
💠Pricing undecided: vegan D3 is 3–4x the price of animal-derived D3, and the new technology allows competing at animal D3 pricing

▫️Complex injectables (dual chamber vials, DCV):
💠Long-term agreement signed 30 Sep 2026 with a leading global pharma company for two DCV products for the US; Symbiotec handles development, regulatory submissions, manufacturing and supply, partner handles commercialisation and distribution
💠Three revenue streams: development and regulatory milestones, product supply (likely from next fiscal year onwards), profit share on partner's sales
💠US addressable market ~US$300 Mn per year for the two products
💠First ANDA filed in September and accepted without RTR; expedited review status received; second ANDA to be filed in Q4 FY27
💠Goal date July; USFDA approval and inspection expected late CY2027; commercialisation expected in FY28
💠Majority of 18–20 Mn vial capacity contracted for the two US products; 3–4 more products in pipeline including one from the partner; 505(b)(2) pathway
💠Discussions ongoing with distributors in Europe and China; milestone payments remain the preferred structure
💠Dual chamber bags and syringes: proof of concept ready; next capex decision (bags, syringes or testosterone injectable CDMO) expected within a quarter or two

▫️Fermentation CDMO — European alternative protein customer:
💠Binding 10-year take-or-pay agreement signed; exclusive arrangement for this customer and product
💠Phase 0: technology transfer and qualification batches at existing 100 KL; ~3 months of fine tuning, production expected within FY27
💠Phase 1: under 1 million litre, 12–15 months to build; 750 KL capacity (revised up from 600 KL)
💠Phase 2: above 1 million litre; few million litres in total across phases at ~₹1,000 Cr, with a significant part funded by the customer
💠Toll fees from Phase 1 in few tens of millions of dollars; FY29 expected to be the best year, no significant Phase 1 revenue in FY28
💠Customer is owned 50% by two European MNCs and 50% by venture capital; product already launched from pilot facility

▫️Fermentation CDMO — US alternative protein customer:
💠10-year take-or-pay; downstream facility in final stages; commercial batches in Q1 FY28
💠Regulatory approvals already in place

▫️Ujjain site: 400 KL fermentation capacity; 32 acres plus 40 acres newly acquired, now a 72-acre site
💠Part of the 400 KL is shared between the US customer, the new European customer and scale-up trials for other prospects
💠Around four customers in advanced discussions for bio-manufacturing

▫️Biologics (insulin drug substance, 14 KL):
💠5-year take-or-pay; commissioning in Q4 FY27 with validation batches; sales from mid FY28 after regulatory approvals
💠Capex a little over ₹100 Cr; delay of a couple of months after a biologics expert joined and standards evolved
💠Only ~50% of capacity given to the current customer; focus on insulin analogs and GLP-1; microbial fermentation, not mammalian

▫️Total gross capex ₹1,509 Cr as of 30 Sep 2026; ₹1,672 Cr estimated by end FY27
💠Over ₹1,000 Cr invested in new businesses, revenue for majority of new capacities already contracted
💠H1 FY27 gross capex ~₹113 Cr
💠Annual corporate capex of ₹200–300 Cr funded from internal cash flows

▫️Premarin (natural conjugated estrogen): API development complete and validation largely done; partner developing formulation, a generic is already in the market
💠Launch a few years away; no commercial impact expected in coming quarters

▫️Testosterone injectable CDMO: exploratory stage

👉Q&A :

💠Significant milestone invoice raised to the partner on 30 Sep; to normalise next quarter

▫️Sustainability of API growth:
💠Price pressure continues across APIs; growth is mainly volume-led with better geography and product mix

▫️Confidence in alternative protein partners:
💠Only partners with product already piloted and sold, capital in hand and take-or-pay contracts with large corporates are signed
💠Customer capital participates in capex; goal is EBITDA-positive projections for partners within 18 months on Symbiotec's unit economics

▫️Contract amounts, customer names and the exact customer capex share: not disclosed due to confidentiality
