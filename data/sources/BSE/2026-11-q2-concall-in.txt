BSE Limited Q2 FY27 concall - Strong H1 growth, options premium dips QoQ

📊 Financial Performance (H1 FY27)

• Revenue up 49% YoY to ₹3,257cr.

• Transaction charges up 60% YoY to ₹2,456cr.

• Operating EBITDA up 55% YoY to ₹2,019cr. Margin at 67% vs 64%.

• Net profit up 53% YoY to ₹1,680cr.

📈 Trading Activity

• Q2 index options premium ADTV at ₹22,531cr vs ₹29,615cr in Q1. First QoQ decline.

• October premium ADTV hit a record ₹35,481cr.

• Cash equity ADTV at a record ₹10,494cr in Q2.

• Investor accounts at 26.8 crore, with 3.9 crore added in a year.

🏛️ Primary Markets & Platforms

• Record 71 mainboard IPOs in H1 raised over ₹84,000cr.

• BSE SME crossed 800 listed companies in October, up from 600 a year ago.

• Star MF processed 24.5 crore transactions in Q2, up 22% YoY.

• MSCI tie-up to build MSCI-linked derivatives. Awaiting regulatory approval.

⚖️ Watchpoints

• Still short of the double-digit cash equity market share goal.

• Futures transaction charges are near nil. A revision will come at the right time.

• Key risks flagged: cyber threats, global turmoil and emerging market outflows.

• All 500 colocation racks allocated. Another 170–190 under construction.

🎯 Management Guidance & Outlook

• Current 67–68% EBITDA margin seen as sustainable.

• IPO pipeline of over 150 companies seeking about ₹1.5 lakh crore.

• No plan to slow technology capex.
