Permanent Magnets Limited
PRE S S & M EDI A RE LEA S E
MUMBAI, O CTOBE R 24, 2026
PERMANENT MAGNETS LIMITED, A SPECIALIST IN ELECTRICAL COMPONENTS AND ASSEMBLIES
FOR APPLICATIONS IN AUTOMOBILES & ELECTRICTY METERS, ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL
RESULTS FOR Q2FY27.
Commenting on the results, Sharad Taparia, Managing
Director, said:
“PML has reported a healthy financial performance in Q2FY27 across key
parameters. Revenue from Operations stood at ₹72.46 Cr, up 49% YoY, driven by
good demand in our EV segment and a pick-up in demand from domestic smart
meter customers. Based on current projections, we expect EV demand to remain at
a similar level for the rest of the financial year.
EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹17.81 Cr, up 159% YoY and EBITDA margin
improved to 25%. Margin improvement can be attributed to a better product mix
from higher EV demand and positive operating leverage from higher business
throughput.
Alloys division revenue increase significantly YoY (H1FY27 vs H1FY26). However
the full capacity could not be utilized due to teething issues at the new furnace. We
expect better utilisation of the new furnace capacity in H2FY27.
In the Relays business, we expect to make pilot supplies in H2FY27, with a material
ramp-up expected in FY28.
For Quantum Magnetics, we commissioned the Phase 2 CAPEX, covering block
cutting, machining, and surface treatment, in October 2026, in line with the Q3FY27
timeline we shared earlier. The product approval process is expected to start soon.
For the rest of the year, our focus is on improving utilisation in the Alloys division,
starting pilot supplies in Relays, and moving customer approvals forward in
Quantum Magnetics.”
